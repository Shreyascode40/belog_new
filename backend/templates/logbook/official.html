Rules & Regulations

  1. Students should maintain the log book regularly and produce it whenever demanded by guide/HOD.
  2. Entries should be made in chronological order with date and signature of student and guide.
  3. All activities, meetings, and decisions must be recorded with proper dates.
  4. Project work should be carried out as per the approved schedule.
  5. Weekly meeting with guide is mandatory and must be recorded.
  6. Any change in topic/scope must be approved by guide and HOD.
  7. Attendance in reviews is compulsory for all members.
  8. Plagiarism in report/paper is strictly prohibited.
  9. Maintain proper versioning of documents and code.
  10. Competition/paper details must be entered with proof.
  11. Term-I and Term-II checklists must be completed before submission.
  12. Sponsored projects must maintain meeting records with company.
  13. Final logbook must be verified by guide and HOD before submission.
